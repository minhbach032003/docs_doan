+LI-05 | Complex purchase approval | WMSD supports a purchase proposal, Store Owner review and the resulting supplier order. Multi-level approval, tendering and vendor-selection workflows are excluded.
+LI-06 | Quotations, promotions, loyalty and CRM | Quotation management, promotional campaigns, discount programmes, loyalty programmes, marketing automation and full CRM are excluded.
+LI-07 | Omnichannel and e-commerce integration | Marketplace order synchronization, public storefront features and omnichannel orchestration are excluded.
+LI-08 | Prediction and automatic pricing recommendations | Demand forecasting, automatic replenishment recommendations and dynamic pricing are excluded. Users enter the final selling price or an externally calculated margin; WMSD only validates configured reference values and shows warnings.
+LI-09 | HRM and payroll | Accounts and roles support operational access only. Recruitment, attendance, payroll and other HR functions are excluded.
+LI-10 | General business policy configuration | Users cannot create or freely edit business policies, approval rules or workflow definitions. Fixed validations and role/operation permissions are specified in the UCs. Maintaining master data and final selling prices does not provide a policy editor.

-These major features address the project GAPs directly; supporting access control and reference-data maintenance are treated as underlying system functions, not separate core business features.
