I. Overall Requirements

[35] Heading2
1. Context Diagram

[36] 
WMSD serves exactly five application roles: Store Owner, Accountant, Inventory Staff, Sales Staff and Administrator. Suppliers and customers are external business parties, not additional application roles. WMSD includes Sales Orders, Outbound Deliveries, picking Transfer Orders, Post Goods Issue, internal invoice issuance with PDF/print output, SMTP order/invoice status notifications, customer and supplier returns, and approval and evidence recording for externally completed customer-return refunds. Master data and manually entered selling prices are included; any margin calculation is performed outside WMSD. Sales payment collection, deposits, receivables, debt, full accounting, statutory e-invoicing and live MISA integration are excluded. A general-purpose function to create or edit business policies is not implemented.

2. Main Business Flow

[40] Heading3
2.1 Replenishment, Procurement and Goods Receiving (BF-01)

[41] 
Trigger: Accountant creates a replenishment request based on warehouse needs for Store Owner review.

[42] 
End condition: The replenishment request is rejected without further procurement, or the approved request results in a purchase order and completed goods receipt. Accepted quantities are recorded in inventory by lot, location and quality status, while outstanding purchase quantities are updated.

[43] 
 

[44] 
Figure 1. Replenishment, Procurement and Goods Receiving — actor/system workflow.

[45] 
Decision handling: A rejected replenishment request cannot proceed to purchase order creation. During receiving, accepted goods are recorded as usable inventory, while defective or discrepant quantities are segregated and documented. Only accepted quantities are posted to usable inventory. Outstanding purchase quantities are updated based on actual receipt records.

[46] Heading3
2.2 Defective Goods Isolation and Disposition (BF-02)

[47] 
Trigger: Inventory Staff identifies defective, damaged or otherwise non-usable goods during warehouse operations and records the defect information.

[48] 
End condition: The defective quantity is isolated and its disposition is approved and processed, with inventory quantities, final stock status and movement history updated accordingly. Rejected disposition proposals remain subject to revision and further review.

[49] 
 

[50] 
Figure 2. Defective Goods Isolation and Disposition — actor/system workflow.

[51] 
Decision handling: Identified defective quantities are separated from usable inventory to prevent unauthorized issuing or movement. If a disposition proposal is rejected, the rejection is recorded and the proposal must be revised before further processing. Only approved dispositions may be posted, and the system records the resulting stock changes and movement history.

[52] Heading3
2.3 Warehouse Transfer and Discrepancy Resolution (BF-03)

[53] 
Trigger: Inventory Staff creates a Transfer Request. Store Owner reviews it; on approval, Inventory Staff creates and confirms the Transfer Order.

[54] 
End condition: The transfer is completed after the dispatched and received quantities are reconciled, and the destination inventory is updated. Transfers with unresolved quantity discrepancies remain open until an approved resolution is recorded and applied.

[55] 
 

[56] 
Figure 3. Warehouse Transfer and Discrepancy Resolution — actor/system workflow.

[57] 
Decision handling: Upon dispatch, the system reduces usable inventory at the source warehouse and records the transferred quantity as In Transit. After receiving, the system compares dispatched and received quantities. Matching quantities allow the transfer to be completed. If a discrepancy exists, the system records the difference and creates a resolution request. Approved resolutions update the relevant inventory balances before closure; rejected or unresolved requests keep the transfer open.

[58] Heading3
2.4 Stocktake and Inventory Adjustment (BF-04)

[59] 
Trigger: Store Owner plans and creates a stocktake session, defining its scope and assigned counters.

[60] 
End condition: The stocktake is completed when no variance is identified, or an approved inventory adjustment is posted for verified discrepancies. Inventory balances and adjustment history reflect the approved results.

[61] 
 

[62] 
Figure 4. Stocktake and Inventory Adjustment — actor/system workflow.

[63] 
Decision handling: Inventory Staff activates counting with an immutable book snapshot, records count/recount attempts and reconciles relevant post-cutoff movements. No discrepancy completes the session without an adjustment. A discrepancy requires an Inventory Staff adjustment request, Store Owner approval and separate Store Owner Clear Difference. Approval does not post stock; only authorized Clear Difference posts the approved version once.

[64] Heading3
2.5 Sales Order Fulfilment and Internal Invoice (BF-05)

[65] 
Trigger: Sales Staff creates a Sales Order and an Outbound Delivery for eligible outstanding quantities.

[66] 
End condition: Inventory Staff confirms picking into staging and posts actual Goods Issue once. Accountant creates and issues the internal invoice for eligible unbilled quantities. SMTP notifications follow committed status events.

[67] 
 

[68] 
Figure 5. Warehouse Issue and Stock Deduction — actor/system workflow.

[69] 
Decision handling: Validate usable stock, lot/location and outstanding delivery quantities. Picking TO confirmation moves stock to staging without deducting the warehouse total. PGI deducts actual dispatched stock once. Invoice creation/issuance must prevent duplicate billing; neither action collects payment or creates a debt ledger.

[70] Heading3
2.6 Customer Return and Returned Goods Classification (BF-06)

[71] 
Trigger: Inventory Staff creates a customer-return request linked to the original sale/issue for Store Owner review before inspection.

[72] 
End condition: Inventory Staff records inspection/classification and confirms accepted returned quantities. Accountant may request a refund; Store Owner approves it; Accountant records externally completed refund evidence. The original invoice remains immutable.

[73] 
 

[74] 
Figure 6. Customer Return and Returned Goods Classification — actor/system workflow.

[75] 
Decision handling: Only accepted quantities are posted according to inspection quality status. Refund eligibility is bounded by accepted return quantities and evidence of the original external payment, less prior or pending refunds. No sales-payment collection or bank transfer is executed in WMSD.

[76] Heading3
2.7 Supplier Return and Inventory Reconciliation (BF-07)

[77] 
Trigger: An authorized user creates a supplier return request for goods that must be returned to the supplier, referencing the original receipt, product, lot, storage location, quantity and return reason.

[78] 
End condition: Inventory Staff records supplier acceptance, actual dispatch and acknowledgement. Store Owner reviews the reconciled result and closes the supplier return. Unresolved differences remain open.

[79] 
 

[80] 
Figure 7. Supplier Return and Inventory Reconciliation — actor/system workflow.

[81] 
Decision handling: Isolate affected stock, record external supplier acceptance and dispatch only eligible quantities. Do not deduct goods again if they never entered owned inventory. Store Owner closure requires reconciled quantities and acknowledgement.

2.4 Reliability and Data Integrity

[682] 
MongoDB is the persistence technology. Stock/document postings require atomic and idempotent outcomes and concurrency checks. Picking confirmation, PGI, receipt, transfer, refund recording and Clear Difference must not repeat their business effect when retried. Preserve posted movement and invoice snapshots.

[684] 
Each active UC maps to one of nine features, its screen, permission row and specification. Enforce permissions at operation and record/warehouse scope. Fixed business rules are implemented through the defined workflows; users do not create or edit arbitrary policies.

[693] 
General-purpose policy creation/editing is outside scope. The application implements only the specified fixed validations, workflow decisions and five-role operation permissions.
