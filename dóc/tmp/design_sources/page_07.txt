@startuml
title 2.3 End-to-End Sales Order Fulfillment and Delivery (BF-03)

scale 1400 width

skinparam shadowing false
skinparam defaultFontName Arial
skinparam activityBorderColor #666666
skinparam activityBackgroundColor #FDFDFD
skinparam activityDiamondBackgroundColor #FFF4D6
skinparam activityDiamondBorderColor #888888
skinparam activityArrowColor #666666

|Sales Staff|
start
:Receive customer purchase request;
:Look up products, prices,
and available inventory;

|System|
:Display product information,
pricing, and available stock
by warehouse and lot;

|Sales Staff|
:Confirm order details with customer;
:Create Sales Order;
:Select products, quantities,
prices, and delivery information;

|System|
:Validate Sales Order data;
:Check available stock
across warehouses;

if (Sufficient stock available?) then (Yes)
    :Display eligible warehouses
    and available lots;
else (No)
    :Display stock shortage
    and available alternatives;

    |Sales Staff|
    :Review available stock;
    :Adjust order quantity,
    warehouse, or delivery schedule;

    |System|
    :Revalidate Sales Order;

    if (Order can be fulfilled?) then (Yes)
        :Confirm available quantities;
    else (No)
        :Save Sales Order as Pending;
        stop
    endif
endif

|Sales Staff|
:Allocate stock by warehouse and lot;
:Reserve stock for Sales Order;

|System|
:Validate lot availability
and reserved quantities;
:Update reserved stock;
:Generate stock allocation details;

|Sales Staff|
:Create Goods Issue Request
from Sales Order;

|Store Owner|
:Review Goods Issue Request;

if (Approve Goods Issue Request?) then (Yes)
    |System|
    :Approve Goods Issue Request;
    :Notify assigned Inventory Staff;
else (No)
    |System|
    :Reject Goods Issue Request;
    :Notify Sales Staff for revision;
    stop
endif

|Inventory Staff|
:Review approved Goods Issue Request;
:Locate reserved stock
by warehouse, lot, and location;
:Pick and prepare goods;
:Check product, lot, quantity,
packaging, and physical condition;

if (Picking discrepancy found?) then (Yes)
    :Record shortage or damaged goods;

    |System|
    :Update picking discrepancy;
    :Notify Sales Staff;

    |Sales Staff|
    :Review alternative stock
    or partial fulfillment;

    |System|
    :Validate revised allocation;

    |Inventory Staff|
    :Complete picking
    for confirmed quantities;
endif

|Inventory Staff|
:Verify prepared shipment;
:Confirm Goods Issue;

|System|
:Generate Goods Issue Note;
:Deduct issued quantities
from the source warehouse and lots;
:Update reserved stock
and inventory transaction history;
:Update Sales Order
fulfillment progress;

|Sales Staff|
:Create delivery batch;
:Arrange delivery schedule
and transportation;
:Record shipment dispatch;

|System|
:Update delivery status
to In Transit;

|Sales Staff|
:Record actual delivery result
and customer handover confirmation;

if (Delivery successful?) then (Yes)
    |System|
    :Update delivery status
    to Delivered;
    :Record delivered quantities;
else (No)
    |Sales Staff|
    :Record delivery shortage,
    rejection, or failed delivery;
    :Arrange redelivery
    or return of undelivered goods;

    |System|
    :Update delivery exception;
    :Track outstanding quantities
    and required follow-up;
endif

|Sales Staff|
:Review Sales Order
fulfillment progress;

|System|
if (All ordered quantities fulfilled?) then (Yes)
    :Update Sales Order
    status to Completed;
else (No)
    :Update Sales Order
    status to Partially Fulfilled;
    :Keep outstanding quantities
    available for follow-up;
endif

:Update sales and inventory reports;
:Notify related staff;

stop
@enduml
