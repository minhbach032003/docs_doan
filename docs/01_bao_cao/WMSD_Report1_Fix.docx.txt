2. Project Purpose
Warehouse Management System Development (WMSD) supports the warehouse and sales fulfilment operations of Cửa hàng gạch ốp lát Tân Thương. It connects purchasing, receiving, inspection, put-away, warehouse transfers, Sales Orders, outbound fulfilment, returns and stocktake, with traceability by product, lot, location and quality status.
The project serves one retail store and its configured warehouse locations. Tile stock is identified by item, lot, tone, actual calibre, quality status and storage position, with consistent box, piece and square-metre quantities. A transfer between configured warehouses is tracked as in transit until the destination records its actual receipt.
The first release supports the nine major features in Section IV. WMSD includes Sales Orders, Outbound Deliveries, picking Transfer Orders, Post Goods Issue, internal invoice issuance with PDF/print output, SMTP order/invoice status notifications, customer and supplier returns, and approval and evidence recording for externally completed customer-return refunds. Master data and manually entered selling prices are included; any margin calculation is performed outside WMSD. Sales payment collection, deposits, receivables, debt, full accounting, statutory e-invoicing and live MISA integration are excluded. A general-purpose function to create or edit business policies is not implemented.

II. Product Background
Cửa hàng gạch ốp lát Tân Thương operates at Phố Tân An, xã Tân An, Yên Dũng, Bắc Giang. Its business covers wall and floor tiles. The store sells to customers who purchase goods for construction, renovation and installation.
The store already uses MISA eShop for product, sales, supplier and customer records, inventory totals and operational reports. Its physical warehouse work is still partly recorded by hand or in separate documents. Staff count stock manually; exact warehouse positions are not maintained in a structured location map; and a total balance does not consistently distinguish lot, tone, actual calibre and goods waiting for inspection, damaged or being transferred. Purchase orders delivered in several trips, receiving discrepancies, returns and stock-count corrections are not linked through one warehouse record. These are gaps in the store's current way of working, not claims that MISA eShop lacks those functions.
Tile stock requires item code, brand, material, dimensions, packaging, lot, tone and actual calibre. Boxes, pieces and square metres must convert according to the product specification. A usable quantity must also identify its warehouse position and quality status; an overall product balance alone does not tell staff what can be picked.
In the current purchasing process, warehouse staff check stock, accounting prepares the replenishment proposal, the owner reviews it, and accounting places the supplier order. The supplier may confirm only part of the requested quantity or deliver on several dates. On each arrival, warehouse staff count and inspect the actual item, lot, tone, calibre and packaging condition. Accepted goods are put away; goods requiring inspection or supplier resolution are separated. Accounting compares the supplier documents with the quantity actually accepted.
During day-to-day warehouse work, staff find stock by product and lot, move it between storage positions, and issue goods against a store request. When goods move between warehouses, dispatch and destination receipt may differ because of shortage or damage. Physical stock counts are made by warehouse area, position and lot; a discrepancy requires recount and a recorded cause before the balance is adjusted. Goods returned from a customer or prepared for return to a supplier are inspected and kept out of usable stock until their disposition is known.

III. Existing Solutions


KiotViet and MISA eShop are two existing retail management solutions relevant to the warehouse operations examined in this project. Both provide established capabilities for product management, purchasing, inventory tracking, stocktaking, transfers, and returns.
1. KiotViet
KiotViet Retail supports purchase ordering, multiple receipts against one order, goods tracking by lot/expiry date for enabled items, stock transfers, stock counts and supplier returns. The assessment below compares its documented retail workflows with the specific physical checks used at Tân Thương; it does not assert that the platform is incapable of configuration or extension.
Website / Link
	https://www.kiotviet.vn
	Target Users
	Small and medium retailers and wholesalers using point-of-sale, purchase, inventory and supplier management.
	Core Features
	POS, supplier purchasing, purchase orders, repeated goods receipts, inventory by item, unit handling, optional lot/expiry tracking, warehouse transfers, stock counts, customer and supplier returns, and retail reports.
	Strengths
	The official purchase-order guide explicitly supports multiple goods receipts for one order and the statuses “Nhập một phần” and “Hoàn thành”. The retail transfer guide records dispatched and received quantities, allows a discrepancy note, and updates warehouse stock. Stock counting compares recorded and actual quantities before balancing.
	Scope fit / limits
	Suitable for standard procurement and stock control. The cited workflow descriptions do not establish one mandatory case-specific record joining tile tone, actual calibre, box-to-piece-to-m² conversion, quality inspection, exact storage position and evidence-based handling of exceptions. This is a project fit gap, not a claim that KiotViet lacks lot control or partial receiving.
	GAP for this project
	GAP-01: Multi-receipt PO tracking exists, but Tân Thương still needs per-arrival accepted/rejected/outstanding reconciliation.
GAP-02: Tile tone, actual calibre and package conversion must identify the same physical stock.
GAP-03: Warehouse inspection outcomes must control which received quantities are usable.
GAP-04: Each tile lot requires its real storage bin and a logged location move.
GAP-05: The store needs evidence across transfer dispatch, handover and destination discrepancy resolution.
GAP-06: Actual issue needs the source request, picked lot, bin and physical quantity.
GAP-07: Customer and supplier returns require a warehouse quality decision before release or dispatch.
GAP-08: Paper/Excel counts need a dated snapshot, investigation and controlled adjustment.
GAP-09: Physical warehouse evidence must be connected across the event trail, separate from retail sales records.
	2. MISA eShop
MISA eShop is the retail platform already used by Tân Thương for product, sales and related records. Its official guides cover product variants, lot/expiry tracking, warehouse storage positions, warehouse receipts and transfers, stocktaking, and customer returns. The project therefore addresses the store's remaining end-to-end physical warehouse workflow, not an assumed absence of those standard functions.
The existence, configuration and edition-specific availability of each standard feature must be checked against the store's own MISA eShop account. The project gaps describe operational requirements that are not currently handled consistently in the store's working process; where a vendor guide does not establish a combined control, this is treated as a fit-to-process question rather than a proven product limitation.
Website / Link
	https://www.misaeshop.vn
	Target Users
	Retail SMEs that need sales, stock, tax/invoicing and related operational records on one platform; this is the case store's current retail system.
	Core Features
	Product variants (including colour/size), lot/expiry tracking, multiple warehouses, structured storage positions (including parent position), warehouse receipts, transfers, stocktaking, retail returns, invoicing and reporting.
	Strengths
	MISA eShop documents creation of named warehouse positions and nested locations; the goods-receipt guide includes warehouse and storage-position fields. Product inventory can be viewed by warehouse, attribute and lot where configured. The inventory-count guide records actual counts, differences and causes; transfer-order and return workflows are also documented.
	Scope fit / limits
	MISA eShop is retained for retail and accounting transactions. Although product attributes, lots, storage locations, transfers and stock counts exist, Tân Thương currently also uses paper/separate warehouse records. The cited instructions do not by themselves verify that the installed configuration enforces a single inspection-to-bin-to-picking trail with case-specific proof at every physical checkpoint.
	GAP for this project
	GAP-01: The store must reconcile each supplier delivery and quality-accepted balance with its purchase record.
GAP-02: Standard attributes and lot tracking must be tied to tile-specific tone, actual calibre and unit conversions.
GAP-03: Receipt inspection must split usable, pending, damaged and rejected quantities by delivery.
GAP-04: Existing storage-position functions need consistent deployment and traceable physical relocations at Tân Thương.
GAP-05: Transfer orders need locally recorded dispatch, handover, arrival and discrepancy evidence.
GAP-06: Warehouse issues need actual picked lot, position and issued quantity against their source request.
GAP-07: Physical inspection and quarantine must precede retail return/accounting settlement.
GAP-08: Count cut-off, recount, cause and review are needed beyond the store's current paper/Excel method.
GAP-09: Warehouse proofs and exceptions must form one event history with MISA document references.
	3. Consolidated Gaps and Section Summary
Gap ID
	Operational mismatch with existing solutions and store practice
	Business impact
	GAP-01
	Partial supplier delivery reconciliation: KiotViet already documents partial goods receipts; MISA provides purchase-related receiving. At Tân Thương, delivered, quality-accepted, defective and outstanding quantities are not currently consolidated by supplier confirmation and each arrival.
	A purchase order can appear settled while goods remain to be received or resolved.
	GAP-02
	Tile-specific identity and unit consistency: The platforms support standard item attributes and lot-related inventory. The store needs its lot, tone, actual calibre, packaging specification and box/piece/m² equivalent recorded and validated together when goods are handled.
	A matching item code can conceal a wrong tile batch or inconsistent stock quantity.
	GAP-03
	Inspection-to-usable-stock separation: Retail receipts exist, but the store also checks packaging, tone, calibre and damage manually. Its separate notes do not consistently connect each inspection outcome to accepted, waiting, damaged or rejected quantity.
	Unverified stock can be presented as available for picking.
	GAP-04
	Position-level stock and movement trace: MISA eShop documents hierarchical storage positions; the case store has not yet maintained a consistent physical position map or logged every move by lot/status.
	Staff cannot reliably locate the intended lot or explain a misplaced quantity.
	GAP-05
	Transfer checkpoints and exception ownership: KiotViet documents dispatch/receipt and difference notes; MISA documents transfer orders. The store needs attributable evidence for dispatch, carrier handover and destination receipt, followed by a documented resolution of short or damaged quantities.
	It is difficult to establish where a transfer discrepancy occurred and who must resolve it.
	GAP-06
	Actual warehouse picking and issue: Existing retail sales and stock-issue functions do not replace the store's need to tie a physical issue request to the exact picked lot, location, quality status and issued quantity.
	The posted issue may differ from what was actually handed over.
	GAP-07
	Warehouse treatment of returns: Both products support returns as retail transactions. Tân Thương needs an independent physical check for customer-return quality and supplier-return quarantine, with accepted and rejected quantities recorded before stock disposition.
	Damaged or unresolved returned goods may be mixed with usable stock.
	GAP-08
	Stocktake cut-off and controlled adjustment: Both products support stock counts and variance handling. The store's described practice involves paper/Excel re-entry and transactions during the count, without a consistently recorded snapshot, recount, reason and review decision.
	Discrepancies cannot be reconstructed reliably after a stock adjustment.
	GAP-09
	Unified physical warehouse audit trail: MISA eShop provides retail documents and reports, but the store's physical inspection, bins, handovers and exception evidence remain partly in separate records. WMSD will keep source-document references rather than duplicate retail or financial processing.
	An individual quantity cannot be followed across receiving, storage, movement, issue and return.
	Section Summary. KiotViet and MISA eShop already offer established retail and inventory functions. The nine GAPs describe the store's unresolved physical controls and fragmented records. WMSD addresses tile-specific inspection, traceable stock events, exception resolution and count accountability, together with Sales Order fulfilment and internal invoicing. Financial accounting and sales payment/debt management remain outside WMSD.
IV. Proposed Solution
WMSD is a responsive web application for Store Owner, Accountant, Inventory Staff, Sales Staff and Administrator. The proposed solution links warehouse execution with Sales Orders, internal invoicing and accepted customer-return refund records. WMSD includes Sales Orders, Outbound Deliveries, picking Transfer Orders, Post Goods Issue, internal invoice issuance with PDF/print output, SMTP order/invoice status notifications, customer and supplier returns, and approval and evidence recording for externally completed customer-return refunds. Master data and manually entered selling prices are included; any margin calculation is performed outside WMSD. Sales payment collection, deposits, receivables, debt, full accounting, statutory e-invoicing and live MISA integration are excluded. A general-purpose function to create or edit business policies is not implemented.
Gap ID
	Proposed solution
	Operational handling
	Major FE
	GAP-01
	Purchase deliveries and outstanding balances
	Record the supplier-confirmed order and each receipt separately. On each visit keep ordered, received, accepted, pending/rejected and unreceived quantities, then reconcile completion only when the remaining quantities are resolved.
	FE-01
	GAP-02
	Tile master / lot / conversion rules
	Store material and dimensions, lot, tone, actual calibre, pack size and controlled conversions between boxes, pieces and m²; validate product specification when goods enter or leave the warehouse.
	FE-02
	GAP-03
	Receiving inspection and stock eligibility
	Register actual receipt quantities, inspect tile attributes and box condition, classify accepted/pending/damaged/rejected quantities and allow picking only from usable stock.
	FE-03
	GAP-04
	Bin-level stock control
	Maintain areas and positions; assign accepted stock by lot/tone/calibre/status and record every physical relocation with source and destination locations.
	FE-04
	GAP-05
	Evidence-based transfer reconciliation
	Record transfer request and owner decision, actual dispatch, carrier handover and destination receipt; retain quantity, actor, time and supporting evidence, keeping differences open until resolved.
	FE-05
	GAP-06
	Sales Order fulfilment and internal invoicing
	Create Sales Order and Outbound Delivery, create/confirm picking TO into staging, and post actual Goods Issue once. Issue the internal invoice for eligible unbilled quantities and send status email after commit. No sales payment collection or debt ledger is implemented.
	FE-06
	GAP-07
	Quality-controlled returns
	Inspect customer returns before re-admitting stock; isolate damaged or supplier-return quantities, then record the physical supplier-return handover against the original warehouse receipt. For accepted customer returns, Accountant requests a refund, Store Owner approves it, and Accountant records evidence of completion outside WMSD. Sales payment collection and debt management remain excluded.
	FE-07
	GAP-08
	Controlled inventory stocktake
	Capture actual counts by location, lot and status against a dated book-stock snapshot; preserve later movements, variance, recount, cause and owner-reviewed adjustment before closure.
	FE-08
	GAP-09
	Cross-process warehouse trace and reporting
	Link warehouse events with source documents, responsible person, timestamp, quantity and evidence; provide operational on-hand and exception reports while retaining manual references to MISA eShop records without live integration.

Step
	Role
	Description
	1. Plan and Create Stocktake Session
	Store Owner
	Define the warehouse, locations, products/lots, schedule and counters, then create the session and assign its scope. The immutable book snapshot is captured when counting is activated, not by overwriting current stock.
	2. Freeze Book Quantity and Physical Count
	Inventory Staff
	Activate counting, retain the book-quantity snapshot and cutoff, and count physical quantities. Post-cutoff movements remain traceable for reconciliation; this is not a blanket lock of all warehouse transactions.
	3. Record Count Results and Reconcile Stock
	Inventory Staff
	Record quantities, units, condition, counter and time, retaining each count/recount attempt and its evidence rather than overwriting earlier results. Compare the selected results with the snapshot and relevant intervening movements. If there is no discrepancy, complete the session without adjustment; otherwise continue to step 4.
	4. Create Adjustment Request
	Inventory Staff
	Submit the reconciled difference, affected lot/location/status, reason and evidence for Store Owner approval.
	5. Approve Adjustment and Clear Difference
	Store Owner
	Review the reconciliation and approve the specific request/version; approval alone does not change stock, and unapproved requests remain unresolved. Post the approved difference once, retaining before/after quantities and later movements, then close the session only after its differences have been resolved.

	Description

	Accountant
	Update the PO to reflect the agreed changes and the items or quantities that can be fulfilled.

	Accountant
	Issue the revised PO to the supplier and return to Step 5 to record the updated fulfilment response.

	18. Close the purchase order
	Accountant

	Description

	Inventory Staff

	5. Inspect and classify goods in WMSD
	Inventory Staff

	Inventory Staff
	Confirm the inspected accepted quantity against the original outbound reference, preserving lot, location and quality status. 	6b. Assign location and confirm put-away
	Inventory Staff
	Accepted goods are stored at the assigned position and placement is confirmed. Goods become sellable only after put-away.
	7. Isolate and record non-usable quantity
	Inventory Staff
	Quarantine, damaged or awaiting-supplier quantities are held in isolation, excluded from usable stock, with a damage dossier where needed. Awaiting-supplier quantities feed the supplier-return flow.
	8. Reconcile return with source and close
	Inventory Staff
	Accepted plus isolated quantities are reconciled to the source; the request is closed.

	2. Limitations & Exclusions
ID
	Excluded Item
	Reason
	LI-01
	Multi-store retail management
	The release serves one store. Warehouse-to-warehouse transfers apply only to configured warehouses operated by that store.
	LI-02
	Sales payment, receivables and full accounting
	WMSD does not collect customer payments or manage deposits, receivables, customer debt, supplier payment ledgers, general ledger or tax statements. For an accepted customer return, WMSD may approve a refund request and record evidence that the refund was completed outside the application.
	LI-03
	Statutory e-invoicing and live MISA integration
	WMSD issues an internal sales invoice with a number, PDF/print output and email status. Tax-authority e-invoice issuance, statutory invoice transmission and live synchronization with MISA remain excluded.
	LI-04
	Fleet and route management
	Transfer and delivery records may identify the responsible carrier, vehicle or handover evidence. GPS tracking, route optimization, dispatch planning and fleet maintenance are excluded.
	LI-05
	Complex purchase approval
	WMSD supports a purchase proposal, Store Owner review and the resulting supplier order. Multi-level approval, tendering and vendor-selection workflows are excluded.
	LI-06
	Quotations, promotions, loyalty and CRM
	Quotation management, promotional campaigns, discount programmes, loyalty programmes, marketing automation and full CRM are excluded.
	LI-07
	Omnichannel and e-commerce integration
	Marketplace order synchronization, public storefront features and omnichannel orchestration are excluded.
	LI-08
	Prediction and automatic pricing recommendations
	Demand forecasting, automatic replenishment recommendations and dynamic pricing are excluded. Users enter the final selling price or an externally calculated margin; WMSD only validates configured reference values and shows warnings.
	LI-09
	HRM and payroll
	Accounts and roles support operational access only. Recruitment, attendance, payroll and other HR functions are excluded.
	LI-10
	General business policy configuration
	Users cannot create or freely edit business policies, approval rules or workflow definitions. Fixed validations and role/operation permissions are specified in the UCs. Maintaining master data and final selling prices does not provide a policy editor.
